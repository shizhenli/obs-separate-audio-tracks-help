<h2>Three routes for this failure</h2>
<div class="product-grid"><div class="product-card"><p class="product-num">A</p><h3>The meter moves but the recording is silent</h3><p>OBS shows audio, but the exported file has no sound. confirm whether the source is muted, the track is unassigned, or the encoder dropped it.</p></div><div class="product-card"><p class="product-num">B</p><h3>The track exists but the wrong audio is on it</h3><p>Tracks are present, but sources are mixed or assigned to the wrong track. map each source to one track and verify with a labelled test.</p></div><div class="product-card"><p class="product-num">C</p><h3>The recording works, but the editor or upload loses tracks</h3><p>OBS file has tracks, but the editor/export/platform drops them. separate OBS output from the editor/platform import behavior.</p></div></div>

<div class="product-callout"><strong>Before the routes: a short precheck.</strong> Confirm the current version, the exact failing task, and a rollback point first. The kit tells you what to record and what a good result looks like before changing a setting.</div>
